About the Artist
Andre Martineau
aka DJ A Wonder

Where beats meet belief.

Born and raised in the heart of Atlanta, DJ A Wonder — Andre Martineau — has spent years perfecting his craft at the intersection of Hip-Hop culture and faith. From club nights to youth ministry events, every set is designed to move the crowd and uplift the spirit.

Operating out of Wonder Work Studio in Atlanta, GA, A Wonder brings premium sound design, live mixing energy, and a unique blend of Trap, Hip-Hop, and Gospel-influenced beats that keep audiences locked in from start to finish.
